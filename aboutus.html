About AniLend

Helping small-scale farmers discover and share useful equipment in their local community.

Why AniLend?

Find tools nearby

Farm equipment can be expensive to buy and may only be needed for part of the growing season. AniLend is a project concept for helping farmers find equipment that local owners are willing to share.

Make idle equipment useful

Owners can share information about equipment they are not using and connect with people who need it. Rental terms and availability should be discussed directly between the people involved.

About this project

AniLend is a community project focused on farm equipment sharing in Bukidnon, helping farmers connect with local owners and discover useful equipment.
